# AGENTS.md - Dan Feliciano Website Rebuild Rules

## Project mission

This website is for Dan Feliciano's business.

The site must speak to business owners and operators who are seeking practical solutions, not to consultants, academics, or enterprise strategy insiders.

The core message is:

Dan Feliciano helps business owners and operators find what is slowing the business down, fix the work, recover lost time, improve follow-up, reduce chaos, and use AI, automation, Lean Six Sigma, analytics, and strategy where they actually create measurable value.

The site must not feel like a private consultant-facing website. It must feel like a practical, owner-facing solution site.

## Master brand

Use only this master brand:

Dan Feliciano

The dissolved legacy brand must not appear anywhere in the deployed website.

Do not include the dissolved legacy brand in visible copy, metadata, schema, image alt text, routes, filenames, comments, fixture data, tests, generated content, or redirects shown to users.

If old assets are reused, absorb them into the Dan Feliciano brand.

Primary audience:

- Small and mid-sized business owners
- Operators
- Local business leaders
- Service business owners
- Professional services firms
- Healthcare administrators
- Public-sector and quasi-public operators
- Owners who feel stuck, overloaded, behind, or unsure where AI belongs

Secondary audience:

- Institutional buyers
- Associations

- Event organizers
- Teams needing training

## Audience mindset

The buyer is not usually thinking:

- I need a strategy consultant
- I need governance
- I need transformation
- I need an AI roadmap
- I need KPI architecture
- I need a strategic operating cadence

The buyer is usually thinking:

- Work is piling up
- I am losing time
- Customers are slipping through the cracks
- My team is overwhelmed
- Everything runs through me
- We are doing too much manually
- I know AI could help, but I do not know where
- We keep fixing the same problems
- I need fewer headaches
- I need the business to run better

Write for that buyer.

## Core positioning

Use this positioning throughout the site:

Fix what is slowing your business down.

Supporting message:

Dan helps business owners and operators find bottlenecks, recover lost time, improve follow-up, reduce chaos, and use AI or automation where it actually makes the business easier to run.

## Messaging hierarchy

Always lead with buyer pain and business outcomes.

Correct hierarchy:

1. Buyer problem
2. Practical outcome
3. Plain-English offer
4. Method or framework
5. Credentials and proof

Do not lead with credentials, frameworks, abstract service categories, or consultant language.

Bad order:

Credentials -> frameworks -> services -> buyer problem

Good order:

Buyer problem -> practical promise -> simple offers -> proof -> method -> credentials

## Voice and tone

The voice should be:

- Plainspoken
- Practical
- Owner-aware
- Strategic but not abstract
- AI-aware but not hype-driven
- Operational but not boring
- Confident but not inflated
- Direct but not condescending
- Smart without sounding academic

The reader should think:

"This person understands what is actually happening inside my business."

The reader should not think:

"This sounds like a consultant talking to other consultants."

## AI messaging rule

Do not present AI as the strategy.

Use this principle:

AI is not the strategy. AI is a tool for fixing work that should be faster, easier, more consistent, or less dependent on manual effort.

Preferred AI message:

Dan starts by finding the work that is slow, repetitive, inconsistent, or too dependent on one person. Then he helps decide whether AI, automation, analytics, process redesign, training, or simple management discipline is the right fix.

Avoid hype phrases like:

- AI transformation
- AI-first revolution
- unlock the power of AI
- future-proof your business with AI
- AI maturity journey
- AI roadmap

Use practical AI phrases like:

- save time
- reduce manual work
- improve follow-up
- automate repetitive tasks
- simplify reporting
- find where AI actually helps
- stop guessing where AI belongs
- automate the right work

## Strategy messaging rule

Do not sell "strategy" as an abstract service.

Use this definition:

Strategy is the reason you say yes to some opportunities and no to others.

Owner-facing explanation:

When every problem feels urgent, the business becomes reactive. Dan helps owners sort the noise, identify what matters most, decide what to fix, what to stop, what to automate, and what to focus on next.

## Offer summaries

Use these summaries consistently.

### Bottleneck Diagnostic

A focused review to identify where work is getting stuck, where time is being lost, and what to fix first.

### Backlog Kill Kit

A practical diagnostic for teams whose work is piling up, taking too long, or falling through the cracks.

### AI Time Saver Sprint

A focused sprint to find repetitive tasks, missed follow-up, reporting work, manual handoffs, and customer communication gaps where AI or automation can save real time.

### 90-Day Operations Reset

A 90-day engagement for businesses stuck in firefighting mode. Stabilize the work, reduce delays, clarify ownership, and install a better operating rhythm.

### Owner Operating System

A simple management system for owners who are tired of chasing everything themselves. Build clearer priorities, better follow-up, simple metrics, decision rules, and a weekly rhythm that keeps the business moving.

### Dan Feliciano Academy

Lean Six Sigma + AI training for teams that need to solve problems, reduce waste, improve service, and use modern tools responsibly.

### Policy Forensics

Decision intelligence for institutions and policy-sensitive organizations that need to understand policy, regulatory, legislative, or public-sector change before it becomes operational risk.

On the homepage, place it below the main owner/operator offers or identify it as:

For institutions, associations, and policy-sensitive organizations.

Do not let Policy Forensics dominate the homepage unless specifically requested.

## Proof rule

Use proof after the buyer pain and practical promise.

Proof should be framed as outcomes:

- reduced delays
- improved follow-up
- simplified reporting
- increased capacity
- improved service flow
- strengthened decision-making
- built problem-solving capability
- improved operating discipline

Credentials may include:

- 25+ years of experience
- GE Six Sigma Master Black Belt background
- experience across healthcare, public sector, finance, manufacturing, logistics, and service operations
- AI, automation, analytics, Lean Six Sigma, and strategy expertise

Do not make unsupported numerical claims unless the data is available in the repository.

## Definition of done

The work is done only when:

- The homepage sounds like it was written for business owners seeking relief and practical solutions.
- The site does not sound like a private consultant-facing website.
- The hero uses the approved owner-facing message.
- The main offers are renamed or reframed in owner language.
- Frameworks appear only as supporting methods.
- The dissolved legacy brand does not appear anywhere in deployed code.
- Main navigation uses practical buyer-facing labels.
- Every main page has owner-facing copy.
- AI is positioned as a tool for saving time and fixing work, not as the strategy.
- Strategy is translated into choices, focus, tradeoffs, and what to stop doing.
- Contact CTAs invite the visitor to find the bottleneck or start a practical diagnostic.
- The site builds successfully.
